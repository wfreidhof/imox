About Imox

Imox is one of the most energetic and entertaining bands you'll ever see. This powerhouse trio brings a dynamic fusion of talent and passion to every stage they play. Featuring Luis de la Rosa (Guatemala) on guitar, Deven Alvarado (Roatán) on drums, and Will Freidhof (U.S.) on bass, Imox delivers a vibrant sound that gets every crowd moving.

Their main stage is on Roatán, but this year they're hitting the road and bringing their unforgettable live show to new audiences.

Book Imox for your next event — and get ready for a night of incredible energy, rhythm, and pure musical connection.
